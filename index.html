Seat-Sync
early concept · seeking feedback

Stop calling test centers one by one to find who has a scribe free on Tuesday.

Test centers that deliver accommodations — private rooms, screen readers, scribes, extended time — have no shared way to show delivery vendors what they can actually support on a given day. Today that means phone calls, one center at a time, until someone says yes. Seat-Sync is an early concept for a registry that fixes that.

No product yet — this is validation, not a pitch.

How it would work

Three parties, one shared record

Centers publish what they can support. Vendors search across centers instead of calling each one. Nothing here replaces how a center takes bookings today — it just replaces the phone tree.

01

Center sets capacity

An owner picks the accommodations their site supports from a standard list and sets how many they have, by date.

02

Vendor searches by need

A scheduler asks for a center near a candidate with private room, screen reader and scribe, all free for the exam window.

03

Match, hold, confirm

Matching centers and times come back instantly. A short hold prevents two vendors booking the same seat.

Who this is for

Two very different daily headaches

If you run a test center

Fewer calls, same control

  • Set what you support once, update capacity in minutes
  • Decide exactly which vendors can see your availability
  • No change to how you already take bookings

If you schedule exam accommodations

One search instead of ten calls

  • Search by accommodation, date, radius and exam length
  • See real availability, not a static list of equipment
  • Hold a slot while you confirm with the candidate
